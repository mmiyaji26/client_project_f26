UMSI Engaged Learning Office

Build Skills. Work with Partners. Have an Impact.

The UMSI Engaged Learning Office (ELO) connects students with opportunities to apply classroom knowledge to real challenges. Use this site to find guidance for the stage of work you are navigating now.

How to Use This Site

Engaged learning projects change as teams learn more. Start with the stage that matches your current needs, then return to earlier guidance when your project changes direction.

  1. Prepare: Set learning goals, understand the context, and examine your assumptions.
  2. Start: Establish relationships, clarify roles, and agree on a project plan.
  3. Work: Manage the project, communicate progress, respond to feedback, and practice ethical engagement.

Explore Engaged Learning Opportunities

ELO supports many ways to apply information skills with organizations, communities, student teams, and partners in Michigan and beyond.

  • AI Clinic Project Experience: Work with a team on an applied artificial intelligence project for a partner organization.
  • A2 Data Dive: Use information and data skills to address challenges identified by community organizations.
  • Alternative Fall Break: Participate in a community-engaged learning experience during fall break.
  • Alternative Spring Break: Collaborate with communities and nonprofit organizations on information-related projects.
  • Study Abroad: Build intercultural awareness and apply your learning in an international setting.

About the Engaged Learning Office

The ELO works with partners across industries and sectors, from technology companies to grassroots nonprofits and cultural heritage institutions, to provide project-based opportunities for UMSI students.

Its mission is to facilitate transformational engaged learning experiences and enable innovative, sustainable, and mutually beneficial information solutions for local and global communities.
