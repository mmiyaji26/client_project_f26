# client_project_f26

Semantic HTML5 deliverable for the SI 539 Fall 2026 UMSI Engaged Learning Office client project.

## Pages

- `index.html` - Home
- `prep.html` - Preparing Your Experience
- `starting.html` - Starting Your Experience
- `during.html` - During Your Experience

The site is intentionally unstyled for this deliverable. Every page includes the same navigation, a Skip to Main Content link, a main landmark, and a footer.

## Content sources

Student-facing content is adapted from the supplied `SI 539 Client Project - ELO Content.pdf` and `539 F26 Client Project - Engaged Learning Office Website.pdf`. HTML comments identify the source material and AI-assisted copy organization, as required by the assignment.
