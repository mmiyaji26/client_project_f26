## CURRENT STATE
The project is a four-page, semantic HTML5 website for the UMSI Engaged Learning Office (ELO). The site is for UMSI undergraduate and graduate students and organizes practical resources around the engaged-learning lifecycle. Deliverable 1 is intentionally unstyled HTML and must include accessible structure, shared navigation, a skip link, images, links, lists, and a footer on every page.
